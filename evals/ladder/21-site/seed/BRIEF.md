# Brief: a site for Ada Okafor

Ada Okafor is a freelance product designer in Lisbon. She works with small teams on
software people use every day -- tools for nurses, ferry timetables, a budgeting app --
and she wants a one-page site she can send to a prospective client: who she is, three
things she has made, and how to reach her.

## Must be on the page

- Her name, once, as the page's main heading, and in the page title.
- A short introduction: what she does, for whom, where she is. Two or three sentences.
- Her portrait, `assets/portrait.svg`, with alt text that says what it is.
- A navigation with links to the three sections: `#work`, `#about`, `#contact`.
- **Work** (`#work`): three projects, each as its own card with the project's name as a
  heading, a line or two about it, and a link to it. The projects:
  - **Tidewatch** -- a ferry timetable for the Tagus crossings that works offline.
    https://example.com/tidewatch
  - **Ledgerline** -- a budgeting app for freelancers with irregular income.
    https://example.com/ledgerline
  - **Field Notes** -- a note-taking tool for district nurses, built with the nurses.
    https://example.com/field-notes
- **About** (`#about`): a paragraph on how she works. She likes to sit with the people who
  will use a thing before she draws anything, and she ships small and often.
- **Contact** (`#contact`): a mail link to `ada@okafor.design`, and the city.

## What a good one does

- Reads well on a phone (390 pixels wide) and on a laptop (1280), with nothing spilling
  sideways.
- Has a light scheme and a dark one, following the visitor's `prefers-color-scheme`, and
  the text is readable in both: at least 4.5:1 against its background.
- Uses system fonts or fonts that ship with the page; nothing is fetched from elsewhere.
- Every image has alt text; the page has one `main`, and headings in order.
- Loads with no errors in the console.

## What she asked for, in her words

"Calm. Not a startup landing page. Room to breathe, one or two typefaces, and a colour I
would actually paint a wall. It should feel like it was made by a person."
